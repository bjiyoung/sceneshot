1. Overview

SceneShot is a Windows desktop application for screen recording, screenshots, and system audio recording, developed by [Publisher name]. This policy explains what information SceneShot does — and does not — collect.

In short: SceneShot does not collect, store, or transmit any of your personal data. Everything it captures stays on your own computer.

2. Information we collect

SceneShot does not send any data to us or to any third party. Specifically:

  • Recordings and captures. Video recordings, screenshots, and audio recordings you make are saved only to a folder on your own device that you choose. We never receive a copy of them.
  • App settings. Your language preference is saved locally on your device (at %AppData%\SceneShot\settings.json) so the app remembers it next time you open it. This file never leaves your device.
  • No accounts. SceneShot does not require you to create an account or sign in.
  • No analytics or telemetry. The app does not include any analytics, crash-reporting, or usage-tracking services.

3. Permissions SceneShot uses

SceneShot requests a few system capabilities to do its job. All of them are used strictly on your device, for the feature described, and never to collect or transmit data:

  • Screen capture — to record or screenshot your display, a window, or a selected region.
  • System audio / microphone loopback — to record the sound currently playing on your PC when you choose to include audio in a recording.
  • Global keyboard shortcuts — F9, F10, and F11 start/stop recording, capture a screenshot, and toggle the on-screen annotation toolbar. While the annotation pen/shape tools are active, SceneShot also watches for the Escape key to return to pointer mode. None of this keyboard activity is logged, stored, or transmitted — it is only used to trigger the corresponding action inside the app itself.

4. Third-party services

SceneShot does not integrate any third-party analytics, advertising, or data-sharing services. If you purchase the full version through Microsoft Store, that purchase is processed entirely by Microsoft — we do not receive or store your payment details.

5. Data sharing

Because SceneShot does not collect any data, there is nothing to share, sell, or disclose to third parties.

6. Children's privacy

SceneShot is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, regardless of age.

7. Data security

Since all recordings and settings stay on your own device, their security depends on your device's own protections (login password, disk encryption, etc.), the same as any other file you create locally.

8. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Continued use of SceneShot after a change means you accept the revised policy.
